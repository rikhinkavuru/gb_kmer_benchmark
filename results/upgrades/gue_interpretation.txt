8. CONCLUSION FOR THE WORDING
   The pipeline and the route rules ran unchanged on all 28 GUE datasets (4 species, binary and multiclass) and gave
   interpretable, falsifiable readouts: the positional route is recovered (splice, both TATA-promoter tasks),
   composition-solvability is common, and a split-design artifact outside the taxonomy is exposed (mouse TF).
   The results do NOT support restoring 'any genomic sequence-classification benchmark':
    (a) GUE is only partly independent: 6/28 datasets are near-disjoint from GB/NT (<= 0.2% shared; mouse_tf_0 9, mouse_tf_1 23, mouse_tf_2 6, mouse_tf_3 2, mouse_tf_4 21, covid none sequences); human TF shares
        19.8%-44.8% of its sequences with the paper's tasks; promoter positives, most splice sequences and the yeast tasks are NT data;
    (b) every failure route fired on data shared with GB/NT (min shared fraction among failure-route datasets 65.8%); on the near-disjoint datasets the rule returned hard (no single route):3; solved:3;
    (b') only 3 of the 12 failure-route labels (the positional ones) are untouched by the post-hoc checks: the 5 yeast Route-1 labels
        become hard when richness is length-normalised (5/5 at 200 bp, 4/5 at 300 bp), and the 4 yeast Route-3 labels fall on measured low-signal windows, not sampled negatives (PH5);
    (c) 8/28 datasets (<= 101 bp) are labelled hard: 7 because the Route-1 richness threshold is unreachable at that length (PH4),
        mouse_tf_3 because its motif AUROC is >= 0.6; Routes 1/3 are defined for binary tasks only;
    (d) on the yeast histone marks the Route-3 signature (5 datasets) tracks genomic location across marks (rho=0.976), but within a mark location explains it for only 1 of 5 (H3K4me1; only partly with 4 strata) and partly for 1 (H3K36me3);
        the rest is an unresolved AT/TATA skew of low-signal windows inside ORFs and intergenic regions;
    (e) the mouse-TF twin artifact is a failure mode the three routes do not name.
   What GUE does support: the unchanged pipeline APPLIES to a separately assembled suite and its readouts are informative
   (same positives + different negatives -> different route). Narrow the abstract/introduction to what was shown.
