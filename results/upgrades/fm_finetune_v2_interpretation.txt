CAVEATS
  * B is above 0.5 where its CI says so (a residual non-composition signal survives); the claim is the share of the score that composition carries, not that the model learns nothing else. Pre-registered rule 1 accepts B<0.6 as 'near chance'; read B's CI directly.
  * Equalized test sets keep few negatives (cohn 228, nt_enhancers 36, drosophila 217; manifest n_neg), so their CIs are wide (nt_enhancers especially); AUROC is prevalence-invariant, and the balanced-resample metrics (20 draws, majority class subsampled) are given alongside.
  * nt_enhancers: 100 released test positives are verbatim training sequences. The released-split A is inflated for every model that memorizes them; Part 2 P1 gives leak-free numbers. The nt equalized split is also not fully composition-balanced (composition-only AUROC 0.559 on its test, manifest), so nt is the weaker of the two unaligned datasets.
  * The drosophila control is GC-balanced (GC-only AUROC 0.491 on its released test) but not dinucleotide-balanced (20-d composition-only AUROC 0.689; cohn 0.764, nt_enhancers 0.786; manifest), so call it a GC-balanced control, not composition-free. A model can therefore lose some AUROC on its equalized negatives too. What separates it from cohn is the smaller share the SAME weights lose on equalized negatives (see the summary), not D: D stays above chance on cohn too for every model except the degenerate fine-tuned HyenaDNA units (fm_finetune_v2_compare.csv D_auroc_mean). Pre-registered rule 5 was NOT met (S_dros's CI overlaps S on released nt_enhancers); its non-overlap criterion was fixed with this caveat stated in advance. The drosophila control is flat for the paper's frozen [SEP] probe only; under mean pooling (frozen or fine-tuned) its GC-matched test effect is negative (Part 3, Q2).
  * Thresholded metrics (balanced accuracy, MCC at p=0.5) depend on calibration: 3 fine-tuning unit(s) put every test sequence on one side of 0.5 (balanced accuracy 0.500, MCC 0; unit_diagnostics one_class). AUROC / balanced-resample AUROC are the informative numbers for such arms.
  * Degenerate fits (Part 3 Q1): hyenadna/human_enhancers_cohn/comp_equalized 3 unit(s). Their D is an optimization collapse of the fixed recipe on the heavily imbalanced equalized train arm, not a measured absence of learnable signal; do not cite it as such. Rules 1-2 do not use D.
  * nt_enhancers epoch selection: 17% of validation rows (mean over units) have an exact copy among the fitting rows, so validation AUROC (mean 0.914) overstates test AUROC (mean 0.740; unit_diagnostics). This is not test leakage (test rows never enter training or selection), but the epoch choice on nt_enhancers is made on a partly memorized carve.
  * Latent positives (Part 3 Q3): the equalizer keeps cCRE-overlapping negatives more often than the random control, so part of S may reflect enhancer-like negatives rather than composition. With every cCRE-overlapping test negative removed the fine-tuned drop survives but is smaller (cohn share lost: DNABERT-2 LoRA-FT 0.24 [0.16,0.34] vs 0.37 unfiltered, B 0.804 [0.762,0.842]; HyenaDNA full-FT 0.56 [0.43,0.70] vs 0.72 unfiltered, B 0.654 [0.604,0.701]).
  * Multiplicity: rules were evaluated on 5 cells without correction; at the Bonferroni level S still excludes 0 in 5/5 (Part 3 Q6).
  * The random arm controls size and imbalance, not every property the equalizer selects on (it keeps negatives whose composition resembles positives; any correlate of that is also removed). The composition-only floor of each arm is listed above for that reason. One random draw (RandomState 42) is used for training and test; psm_sensitivity shows the k-mer spread over 10 draws.
  * Two FMs of modest size (0.44M; DNABERT-2 118.5M incl. LoRA + head; fm_finetune_v2_runs.csv) with one fixed recipe each; absolute AUROC is not tuned to a leaderboard. DNABERT-2 is LoRA (backbone frozen, 1.25% of weights trainable), not full-parameter; its 512-token context excludes the 2-3 kb drosophila sequences, so the GC-balanced drosophila control is HyenaDNA only. DNABERT-2 full-parameter and NT-v2-500M LoRA are packaged for a CUDA GPU (gpu_deferred/, external/fm_finetune/gbkb_ft_gpu_bundle.tar.gz).
  * Not under-trained, but not leaderboard-tuned (post hoc check against published numbers): on the balanced cohn test, fine-tuned HyenaDNA-tiny reaches accuracy 0.716 vs 0.742 published for the same 436k-parameter HyenaDNA architecture after a hyperparameter sweep (HyenaDNA paper, arXiv:2306.15794, Table 4.1); DNABERT-2 LoRA reaches 0.764. On nt_enhancers its MCC is 0.428 vs 0.626 published for the larger 1.6M-parameter HyenaDNA (Table 4.2), so the nt_enhancers HyenaDNA numbers are below a tuned model's.
  * The baseline (k-mer, frozen) rows are post hoc, single-seed, and trained on the full train arm; the fine-tuned models train on a 90% carve (10% validation for epoch selection).
  * The v1 numbers cited in the June/v2 PSB manuscript and numbers_ledger.md (0.752 -> 0.514) came from the defective batcher; the v2 re-run gives 0.796 -> 0.581 (fm_finetune_v2_v1_vs_v2.csv). Replace them with the v2 numbers.
  * Memory (2026-09-24): after a drosophila unit grew to 17.6 GB (per-batch tensor shapes -> unbounded MPS graph cache), the remaining 24 unit(s) ran with fixed-shape padding (--pad-multiple), exact micro-batch gradient accumulation for drosophila (--micro-batch 4; optimizer batch still 32) and hard memory caps (memguard.py). These are numerical no-ops (tests/check_pad_invariance.py: logits <= 9e-8, gradients <= 1.4e-4 relative); per-unit settings and peak footprint in fm_finetune_v2_runs.csv (max peak 3630 MB).
