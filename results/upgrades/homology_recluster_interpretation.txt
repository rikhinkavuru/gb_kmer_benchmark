METHOD. MMseqs2 18-8cc5c all-vs-all nucleotide search, BOTH strands (--strand 2), pooled train+test per task;
edge = fident >= T over >= 80% of both sequences; clusters = connected components (single linkage).
T = 0.8 (primary, conventional nucleotide near-duplicate level) and T = 0.5 (strict: all MMseqs2-DETECTABLE homology,
which in practice means >= ~70% identity because 11-mer seeding limits recall below that; see calibration).
Synthetic calibration (recall of planted pairs, mean over strands; 0/400 random decoys merged): 90%: id80 1.00 id50 1.00; 85%: id80 1.00 id50 1.00; 80%: id80 0.68 id50 0.97; 75%: id80 0.15 id50 0.96; 70%: id80 0.00 id50 0.77; 65%: id80 0.00 id50 0.31
linclust calibration (calibration_linclust.csv, linclust (kmer-per-seq 100); id80, mean over strands): 95%: 0.94; 90%: 0.81; 85%: 0.69; 80%: 0.25; decoys merged 0/400
linclust calibration (calibration_linclust.csv, linclust (kmer-per-seq 21); id80, mean over strands): 95%: 0.94; 90%: 0.79; 85%: 0.62; 80%: 0.23; decoys merged 0/400
-> search + connected components recovers >=85%-identity pairs that linclust misses; it was affordable for every task.

7. CAVEATS
   * Bootstrap CIs resample test sequences, not homology components; within-test families make them somewhat narrow.
     Component-level bootstrap (audit_clusterboot.csv): CI width ratio component/sequence median cluster_minus_random 1.15 (max 11.5), original_mcc 1.10 (max 2.7), purge_mcc 1.00 (max 1.2), resplit_cluster_mcc 1.11 (max 15.0), resplit_random_mcc 1.10 (max 2.8); cluster-minus-random calls that change: demo_human_or_worm id80 -0.009 seq [-0.017,-0.002] comp [-0.020,+0.000]
   * Exact twins MMseqs2 cannot seed (mostly-N or < 11 bp; audit_task.csv, id80): demo_human_or_worm 644 test seqs missed (median 200.0 bp), purge MCC 0.908 -> 0.905 when also removed; 716 re-split test seqs keep an exact train twin; human_enhancers_ensembl 316 test seqs missed (median 15.5 bp), purge MCC 0.427 -> 0.411 when also removed; 314 re-split test seqs keep an exact train twin; human_ocr_ensembl 2 test seqs missed (median 257.5 bp), purge MCC 0.400 -> 0.400 when also removed; 3 re-split test seqs keep an exact train twin
   * Multiplicity: 15 tasks x 2 thresholds x 2 protocols of inflation calls, uncorrected; the pre-registered rule is the call.
   * The 0.05 materiality cut and the prevalence-standardised purge MCC for labels were chosen after results were seen.
   * Coverage >= 80% of BOTH sequences cannot link contained loci of very different length (see containment sensitivity).
   * The remaining paper experiments (Route-3 cleaning arms, specificity panel, composition-only baseline on all tasks,
     frozen-FM probe, graded positional rescue, LR floor, model seeds, motif presence, coordinates) are re-run on
     homology-clean data by run_extra.py, and end-to-end HyenaDNA fine-tuning by run_stage_ft_hc.sh (DNABERT-2 LoRA deferred);
     section 8 summarises them, homology_recluster_extra_interpretation.txt has details.
