CAVEATS: (1) matched and random arms change the test set, so within-arm comparisons are unpaired; the
fixed-model and weighting designs are the paired/removal-free complements. (2) nt_enhancers' test split has
200 negatives, so its alpha=1 test keeps only 36 negatives (wide CIs), and at alpha=1 its matched set is NOT
balanced (max|SMD| 0.299; composition-only AUROC on it 0.640 uncorrected / 0.564 leak-free test; the published nt split max|SMD| 0.286).
(3) nt_enhancers LEAKAGE: 100 of its 200 test positives occur verbatim (same label) in the training split and
are always kept by negative-side selection, so every uncorrected nt number is inflated; the leak-free rows
(nt_enhancers_leakfreeTest = same models, those 100 test positives removed; nt_enhancers_leakfreeRefit = refit
without the 107 training copies) are the ones to report. (4) Weighting balances the 20 features only in
expectation (overlap weights: exact mean balance for the linear propensity terms); nonlinear composition
structure survives (composition-only LightGBM AUROC under weighting), so weighting does not 'equalize' composition.
(5) The within-arm verdict uses selection seed 42 only (pre-registered); the seed range and the published split
are printed next to it. (6) MCC at the 0.5 threshold is prevalence- and calibration-dependent: on a
~94%-positive test set a model trained on ~96% positives predicts almost everything positive, so its collapse
is reproduced by size-matched RANDOM negatives and is not evidence of shortcut removal; even class-balanced MCC
at the prior-corrected threshold falls mostly through the size/imbalance part. Use AUROC against the random
control, and MCC only from the balanced 1:1 design. (7) Composition matching removes composition-associated
signal indiscriminately, artifactual or biological, and not completely (the composition-only AUROC on the
published cohn split is 0.581 [0.544,0.624], from 0.779); calling the removed signal a 'shortcut' rests on
the paper's independent evidence (dinucleotide-shuffled negatives, control-motif panel, genomic localization).
The clean control also loses AUROC under matching; in the paired designs (original model or original test
held fixed) that loss follows the control's own composition-only loss (rule and numbers in (B) above), so
matching removes composition-associated signal from the clean control too, and a matched-vs-random loss is
not by itself evidence of an artifact. It loses a smaller share under overlap weighting, but run_posthoc.py P6 shows that this
contrast is largely accounted for by how much composition-only signal the same weights remove (fully on the
additive scale; see the READING lines for the ratio scale), and even a greater per-unit reliance would not
separate artifact from biology, so it is NOT evidence of specificity to an artifact. (8) Selected negatives are
not a random sample (part (6)). Common-support trimming (F2) keeps every positive but only negatives inside the
positives' central propensity band, so it both removes composition signal (in the band) and creates it (for the
positives outside the band; P8). In the clean control at the strictest levels the RETRAINED k-mer AUROC falls while
the retrained composition-only AUROC does not, but with the original model or the original test held fixed both
fall together (f2_selection_difficulty, per design). So F2 does not isolate difficulty unrelated to composition;
it shows that a propensity-based selection can change a task in ways mean balance does not reveal, which is why
cleaned splits must be benchmarked against size-matched random negatives, in a paired design.
(9) The label-noise figure is a mixture-model estimate, not a worst case.
For nt_enhancers it is 2.97x (nt_enhancers, verdict NOT SUPPORTED), 0.44x (nt_enhancers_leakfreeTest, verdict NOT SUPPORTED), 0.28x (nt_enhancers_leakfreeRefit, verdict NOT SUPPORTED) the matched-vs-random effect; the nt ELS enrichment behind it has Bonferroni p 1 and the nt composition-matching effect is not supported in any variant, so for nt the check says little either way (label noise is neither shown nor excluded).
(10) Overlap weights come from a LINEAR logistic propensity model. Where the composition-only LightGBM
scored under the same weights falls below 0.5, the weights invert the nonlinear composition signal, so that
weighted k-mer AUROC overstates the effect of removing composition and only its direction is interpretable.
