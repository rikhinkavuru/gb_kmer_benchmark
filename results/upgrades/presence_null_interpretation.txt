Reading.
  * The GB-only value reproduces the paper's -0.03 exactly (rho=-0.0333), so the
    published point estimate is correct as stated.
  * But its 95% CI spans essentially the whole admissible range: Fisher
    [-0.68, +0.65], BC bootstrap [-0.73, +0.68]. At n=9 the data are
    compatible with a strong correlation in EITHER direction. The reviewer is right that
    'uncorrelated' overstates what n=9 supports; the defensible claim is that no
    association is DETECTABLE, not that none exists.
  * Adding the six NT tasks (NT-only rho=-0.371) leaves the pooled estimate at
    rho=-0.245 (p=0.38), i.e. still indistinguishable from zero, and the
    pooled CI is still wide: Fisher [-0.67, +0.31],
    BC bootstrap [-0.65, +0.30].
  * So n=15 leaves the null INTACT: it does not reverse and it does not turn positive.
    The point estimate drifts from -0.03 to -0.24, i.e. slightly NEGATIVE (more
    enriched motifs, if anything marginally LOWER MCC -- the direction the paper's own
    'the failed enhancer/OCR tasks are the most motif-rich' observation implies), but at
    every n the interval covers 0 comfortably. What n=15 changes is only that the null must
    be stated as an absence of DETECTABLE association at low power, not as a demonstrated
    absence of association.

Caveats.
  * The enrichment counts were computed at the OLD selection-on-test best k, which differs
    from the validation-selected k on 5 of the 13 tasks that have enrichment rows
    (column enrich_k vs best_k; the 2 zero-enrichment tasks carry enrich_k=-1 because no
    row records their k). Pairing the counts with the k-consistent max-over-k MCC instead
    gives the sensitivity rows in presence_null_sensitivity.csv; the GB value there is the
    -0.23 printed in results/motif_interpretation.txt. All are near zero, none significant.
  * n_enriched_TFs is not comparable in an absolute sense across suites (different
    sequence lengths, class counts and JASPAR collections), so the pooled n=15 test is
    the least assumption-free of the three; the suite-partial rho is in the sensitivity CSV.
  * Tasks are not independent replicates (overlapping human regulatory annotation), so the
    effective n is if anything below 15 and the CIs above are optimistic, not conservative.
